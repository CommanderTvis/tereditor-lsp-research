Does a language server make a Claude Code agent better, faster or cheaper?

Results of the confirmatory run: 2,232 runs, 87 tasks, 29 projects, three Claude models, five arms.

Headline

Across 87 tasks, the optional arm changed success by +0.3 points (interval -2.1 to +3.0) and cost 1.02 times the baseline. The enforced arm changed success by -2.0 points (interval -5.2 to +1.0) and cost 1.57 times the baseline. The baseline-review arm cost 2.34 times the baseline.

Total spend recorded in the runs was USD 243.02. Runs excluded by the contamination check: 0.
